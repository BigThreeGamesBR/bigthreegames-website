How to read it

The numbers are the point. Everything else is there to let you check them.

What each column is claiming

Effects were authored in prototype wording and are restated here in the words the game now uses. Cut Classes keep their original wording, because there is nothing to restate.

TermWhat it means here
PowerA card’s current number, after every active effect. What the corner of the card shows. Never attack.
Base PowerA card’s Power before any effect. A property of the Class, not of the Board — the Base Power column above.
Total PowerThree cards’ Power summed. What decides a Turn.
GainAny effect that raises a card’s Power. Direction only — it does not mean good: under an Inverted Dungeon a Gain works against you.
DrainAny effect that lowers a card’s Power. Direction only — it does not mean bad.
ClassOne character, and the thing a Vinculum is formed from. Unlocked once by playing, then held in unlimited copies.
VinculumThe bond formed by holding enough cards of one Class in a Deck. It has a Tier, and it grants that Class’s effects.
TierA Vinculum’s strength band — Bronze, Silver or Gold. Each Class states how many copies reach each one.
SingleThree Turns. Won by taking two of them. The prototype called this a match.
TurnOne commitment of three cards into three Slots, and the reveal that resolves it.
SlotOne of three positions a card is committed to. Slots pair one-to-one with the opponent’s, and some effects act across that pairing.
HandThe cards still unplayed in this Single. Not a drawn subset — there is no draw, and every card is playable at any point.
Other ClassesEvery card that is not of the Class naming the effect. What a Support Class acts on. Turn-scope is said explicitly instead — played this Turn.
DungeonOne condition applied to a whole Single, announced before the player commits anything.
MonsterA per-Turn condition taxing specific Classes. Three per Single, one live each Turn.
BesideIn the same Turn, in another Slot. The scope word for the other cards you committed this Turn.
Facing cardThe opponent’s card in the Slot paired with this one. Slots pair one-to-one, and facing is always the opponent’s.
PennantThe banner belonging to a Deck, carrying its Class icon and Special Tokens. Hidden from the opponent — unless a Vinculum effect opens it.
Threshold

How many copies buy a tier

A Deck of nine has to spend slots to reach Bronze, Silver or Gold. A 0 means that tier grants nothing — Monk and Rogue sit outside the usual bands on purpose.

Effect

When it fires, then what it does

Each effect opens with its kind. (a/b/c) is per Turn — Turn 1, Turn 2, Turn 3 — and the values do not accumulate. Other Classes means every card not of that Class. Turn-scope says so explicitly — played this Turn.

Status

Whether it is being built

Enabled means a developer could build it without asking a design question. On hold is wanted, with a named question in the way. Cut cannot exist without changing a pillar or a settled decision. Never written has numbers but no effect.

Effect kindWhen it fires
On Start Once, at the start of a Single, after the Board is shown
On Turn At the start of each Turn
Continuous Always, while the Vinculum is active
On Condition Only when its condition is met
On Reveal Only when the Reveal is what makes it knowable. An effect that could be computed the moment cards are committed is On Condition instead

Four things this table cannot answer

The order several Vinculums resolve in

A Cleric buffs Other Classes and a Warlock drains them. Which lands first changes the number on the card — and the design promises the player can account for every point of it. No order is written down.

What Carry and Support actually mean

Across all 26 entries, every Support acts on Other Classes and every Carry acts on its own Class. That is an observation over the data, not a definition anybody has settled.

Three Musketeers’ shape

One Class carrying three different Base Powers, where Base Power is defined as a property of the Class. Its threshold of three also means one of each rather than any three, which no other Class does.

Whether Blood Hunter should exist

Its Type, Base Power and thresholds were authored. All three effect columns were left blank. It is listed so the gap is visible rather than mistaken for an oversight.
