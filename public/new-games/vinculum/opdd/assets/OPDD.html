VINCULUM

Genre  Strategy Card Game Mode  Singleplayer + Pseudo Async Multiplayer Version  1.0 Draft
One Page Design Document

Vinculum is a strategy card game where players build a 9-card hand (Deck) and compete across 3-turn matches inside structured tournament formats. Victory comes from creating Vinculums with the cards, managing Attack Values under Monster debuffs, and reading Dungeon conditions before committing cards.

Cards and Classes

Each card has two attributes: a Class and an Attack Value (AV). Having cards of the same Class increases their Vinculum, leading to better effects.

There are currently 8 active Classes, drawn from a full design pool of 24 Classes, leaving significant room for expansion.

Every Deck carries one special Token that adds +2 to the count of a chosen Class. This allows a player to push a Vinculum level without spending an extra card slot.

Vinculum System

Each Class has up to three levels of Vinculum. When a Deck's Class count crosses a threshold, the corresponding Vinculum activates and applies its effect every turn from that point on.

Match Structure

Every match is played over 3 turns. To win the match, a player must win 2 out of 3 turns. Each turn, both players play 3 cards; the player with the higher combined AV wins that turn.

A turn can end in a draw if AV are equal. The match is only considered a draw when all 3 turns are draws AND the combined AV difference across all turns is also 0. If both conditions are met, the match is decided by a Coin Flip.

Matches can be played as Best of N depending on the tournament format.

Match Flow

Every match takes place inside a Dungeon, a drawn card that establishes a condition affecting the full match. Three Monsters are drawn at match start, one per turn, each reducing the AV of specific Classes during their active turn.

  1. 1
    The Dungeon is revealed to both players. All 3 Monsters are drawn. Only the Turn 3 Monster is shown; Turns 1 and 2 remain hidden.
  2. 2
    At the start of each turn, the current Monster is flipped. Its AV penalty applies to its targeted Class for that turn only.
  3. 3
    Each player plays 3 cards. Vinculums are resolved and all card effects are applied.
  4. 4
    Combined AVs are compared. The turn winner is recorded and play advances.

Singleplayer and Tournaments

The singleplayer loop is built around Tournaments. There are TBD tournaments, each with its own brand and ruleset. Available formats include Swiss + Playoffs (no Lower Bracket), Group Stage + Playoffs (with Lower Bracket), Playoffs only, and others.

Players have an Account Level (AL) that increases through tournament play, unlocking fixed content such as new Classes, Displays, and other items.

Tournament wins, win streaks, and other achievements unlock Card and Token customizations and special Display customizations for the player's Vinculum Display.

Pseudo Async Multiplayer

Vinculum uses a pseudo async multiplayer model to simulate real opponents without live matchmaking. Tournament opponents are BOTs driven by real player behavior data. Each BOT carries a Profile Tag, derived from actual player patterns, visible in the bracket so the player has a read on their opponent before the match begins.

For multiplayer, players can invite friends to a tournament or a friendly match. A full real-time competitive mode is outside the current scope.
